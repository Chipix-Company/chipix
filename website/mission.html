Our mission

An open ecosystem for an industry built on closed IP.

Chip design and verification grew up behind expensive licenses and proprietary stacks. We believe open UVM, open tools, and open systems are the next revolution in the chip industry — and Chipix exists to make that path real.

The industry today

If you want to build software today, your tools are free. Compilers, editors, operating systems, version control — the stack is open, and nobody questions it.

If you want to build and verify a chip, the story is different. Design and verification tooling sits behind licenses from a handful of vendors. Seats cost tens of thousands of dollars a year. A student can't afford a full verification environment. A two-person startup burns runway before tapeout. Even large companies ration tool access to whoever's budget covers the seat.

Much of the industry still runs on closed IP, closed flows, and black-box tools between you and your own results. That model worked for a generation. It does not scale to the next one — when more people, more startups, and more open hardware projects need to verify silicon.

What we believe

Access to good tools shouldn't depend on the size of your purchase order. Every engineer — at every company, at every stage — should be able to sit down and verify a design.

That means an open ecosystem: inspectable software, open EDA you already trust, open UVM and open verification stacks you can read, modify, and share. No seat counting that locks knowledge behind a PO. No black boxes between you and evidence that your design works.

  • Open source studio you can inspect, fork, and self-host
  • Open tools first — Verilator, Yosys/SymbiYosys, Icarus, Slang, and open UVM paths
  • Human-approved changes and local-first execution, including models that never leave your machine

Why open UVM and open systems matter

Software had its revolution when the toolchain went open. Hardware is next — but only if verification escapes proprietary lock-in the same way compilers and kernels did.

Open UVM and open systems let students learn on real flows, startups ship without burning cash on seats, and teams at every scale share methods instead of reinventing closed environments. That is not a side quest. It is the next revolution in the chip industry: collaboration and evidence over gated IP and opaque tools.

What Chipix does

Chipix is the world's first open-source, AI-native studio for chip design and verification. You bring a spec and RTL; an agent helps you understand the design, plan verification, generate unit sims, formal properties, and UVM environments — then runs them on open tools and collects evidence, with you approving every meaningful change.

  • Thread-first workspace: intent, mental model, plans, and results in one place
  • Staged verification on your machine — recommend, plan, approve, execute
  • BYOK AI you control; audit trail your team can trust
